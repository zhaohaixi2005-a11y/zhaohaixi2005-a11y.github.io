Research Question

How can a team deliver a reproducible NOX directed-evolution project under competition deadlines? I led this project while balancing experimental rigor with strict deadlines and deliverables.

Motivation

Under competition deadlines, integrating multistage experimental workflows can expose bottlenecks in construct generation and protocol reproducibility.

Approach

  • Coordinated project planning across experimental design, data analysis, and presentation of the findings.
  • Diagnosed workflow bottlenecks and prioritized improvements in construct generation and protocol reliability.
  • Kept documentation and presentation milestones on schedule.

My Contributions

  • Led team decisions and coordinated resources.
  • Aligned the technical work with competition judging criteria and communication goals.
  • Brought separate tasks together into a coherent project with coordinated deliverables.

Progress and Outputs

  • Delivered an integrated directed-evolution project within the competition’s deadlines and requirements.
  • Improved the reliability of the team’s workflow and strengthened its attention to experimental reproducibility.
  • Received Second Prize in the national undergraduate life sciences competition.

Methods and Tools

  • Project leadership in interdisciplinary research teams
  • Troubleshooting experimental workflows and managing risks to data quality
  • Scientific communication and presentations organized around the evidence

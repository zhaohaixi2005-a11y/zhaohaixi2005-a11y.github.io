Project Gateway 02

De Novo Design of PTM Capture Binders

Computational design of all-helical pseudocyclic binders for lysine-linked LCA (Lys-LCA), using shape complementarity and geometry-aware filtering to prioritize capture candidates.

Dec 2025 - Present Research Intern

Academic Guidance

Institution PEKING UNIVERSITY
Supervisor
Prof. Chu Wang
Lys-LCA PTM BoltzGen RFdiffusion LigandMPNN

Research Question

Can a de novo binder pocket recognize Lys-LCA through shape complementarity and support selective enrichment of modified species for subsequent site characterization?

Motivation

Capturing a specific modification requires a pocket with an appropriate geometry and a stable supporting scaffold. All-helical pseudocyclic architectures provide a way to constrain pocket shape while designing the surrounding protein.

Approach

  • Design all-helical pseudocyclic backbones with topological closure and pockets shaped around the Lys-LCA target.
  • Integrate BoltzGen, RFdiffusion, and LigandMPNN for backbone generation, topology control, and sequence optimization.
  • Filter computational candidates for structural quality, helix packing, and binding pocket formation.
  • Screen and rank candidates in silico for downstream experimental evaluation.

My Contributions

  • Designed the all-helical pseudocyclic binder architecture and built the computational generation pipeline.
  • Implemented geometry-aware filters linking scaffold quality, helix packing, and pocket formation.
  • Established the in silico screening and ranking workflow for candidate prioritization.

Current Progress

  • A computational pipeline is in place for generating and prioritizing Lys-LCA capture-binder candidates.
  • Current outputs are designed structures and ranked computational candidates.
  • Candidates are prioritized for downstream evaluation of binding, selectivity, and capture performance.

Tools

  • BoltzGen, RFdiffusion, and LigandMPNN
  • Pseudocyclic topology control and protein geometry analysis
  • Helix-packing and pocket-quality filtering
